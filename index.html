About

I am an oceanographer with a research focus that runs from the mangrove sediment surface down to the processes that move carbon and nutrients through coastal ecosystems. My work sits at the intersection of field biogeochemistry, geospatial analysis, and numerical modelling — sampling in the field, then bringing that data into R, Python, ArcGIS, and Delft3D to understand how Bangladesh's mangroves, estuaries, and offshore habitats function and change.

Across research assistantships and a national fellowship-funded MSc thesis, I've studied bioturbation and carbon cycling in mangrove ecosystems, oyster habitat relationships, microplastics in estuarine sediments, and marine biodiversity mapping — always with an eye toward conservation-relevant, community-grounded science. I'm now looking to extend this work through further research and applied coastal management practice.
